Chess

Our chess course provides a comprehensive and engaging learning experience for both beginners and intermediate players. Following a structured curriculum, students will build a strong foundation in the fundamentals of chess, including the rules, piece movements, and essential strategies. They will progress to exploring tactical combinations, positional play, and endgame techniques designed to strengthen overall gameplay.

The course places strong emphasis on developing critical thinking, pattern recognition, and decision-making skills. Students will also learn how to create a personalized opening repertoire, prepare effectively for tournaments, and understand the psychological dimensions of the game.

Photography

Students will be introduced to digital photography and the seven principles of photography. They will learn how to operate a DSLR in Manual Mode by understanding the three elements of the exposure triangle—shutter speed, ISO, and aperture—their functions, and how to achieve proper exposure in a photograph. Students will also explore different genres of photography, including motion, product, portrait, and stop-motion photography, while developing the skills to effectively balance the exposure triangle.

Game designing

At Think n Innovative, Game Designing Courses for Kids ignite creativity and introduce young learners to the exciting world of coding and animation. Through engaging, hands-on sessions, children learn to create interactive stories and games while developing essential problem-solving skills.

They explore concepts such as event handling, movement blocks, and animation, bringing their characters to life with costumes, dialogue, and text-to-speech features. Kids gain an understanding of variables, loops, randomization, and Cartesian coordinates, enabling them to control sprite positions and craft dynamic gameplay.

Their courses also teach children how to design stages with multiple sprites and backgrounds, adjust movement speeds, and incorporate add-ons to enhance their projects. Advanced activities, such as creating a Solar System animation using broadcast messages, blend storytelling with STEM learning. By the end of the course, children not only build technical skills but also cultivate creativity, logical thinking, and a passion for innovation in a fun and supportive environment.
